### Biography

Christopher (CJ) Giacomo Yoshi Calabrese, PhD, MPH, is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Communication at Clemson University and a Faculty Scholar in the Clemson University School of Health Research. His research examines how communication interventions can improve decision-making in digital information environments, with an emphasis on health communication, misinformation, artificial intelligence, and persuasive technologies.
