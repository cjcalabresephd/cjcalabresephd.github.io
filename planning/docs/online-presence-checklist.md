# Canonical Biography (Long)

Christopher (CJ) Giacomo Yoshi Calabrese, PhD, MPH, is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Communication at Clemson University and a Faculty Scholar with the Clemson University School of Health Research. His research examines how digital information environments and communication interventions influence decision-making, health behavior, and societal outcomes. He studies digital media, misinformation, and artificial intelligence to develop communication strategies that promote health.
